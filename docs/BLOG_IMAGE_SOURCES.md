# Tamar Homes — Blog image research and attribution

Updated: 2026-11-08.

## Purpose
Images in the blog are **editorial illustrations**, not photographs of Tamar Homes stock, completed projects, listed properties or clients. They must not be reused as listing or hero inventory assets.

The existing six blog posts were reviewed before selecting imagery. The Nigerian title documentation piece was cross-checked against the Lagos State land administration portal and Land Use Act. Other pieces were matched to imagery illustrating their existing subject matter. No unsupported market price/rental yield claims were added.

## Rights and implementation
- License: https://unsplash.com/license — commercial website use is permitted under the standard Unsplash License; attribution is appreciated.
- Links use high-quality, responsive-friendly Unsplash CDN URLs (1600px width, quality 85) and maintain an image placeholder fallback in case a remote request fails.
- Each article shows a photographer/source credit and a clear editorial-only disclosure below the image.
- The photo of Lekki–Ikoyi Link Bridge is *actually from Lagos*; other editorial images are **not** claimed to be from Nigeria.
- Keep third-party images out of property listings; inventory photos must remain owner-provided.
- Browser/network availability and build tests require validation before merging to main.
