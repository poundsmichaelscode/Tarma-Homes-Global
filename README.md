# Tarmar Homes Property Collective

You are a senior full-stack software engineer, UI/UX designer, and real estate product architect. create a real estate  TARMAR HOMES, a premium real estate property sales website. Use primary purple #7138A8, deep purple #4D247A, leaf green #79A942, dark green #426D2E, white #FFFFFF and light background #F8F8F6. Remove all Airbnb branding, booking, guest, host, short-let, check-in/out, nightly pricing and reservation functionality. Build a responsive professional website with Home, Properties, About Us, Services, Blog, FAQ, Contact Us and List Your Property navigation. Build a homepage with property search, featured listings, categories, company introduction, services, testimonials, blog posts, newsletter and footer. Create a searchable/filterable property catalogue and dynamic individual property pages with unique SEO metadata, image gallery, specifications, description, amenities, location, documentation, WhatsApp enquiry, inspection scheduling and related properties. Build About, Services, Blog listing, dynamic blog article pages, Contact, FAQ, Privacy Policy, Terms, Cookie Policy, Disclaimer and Legal pages. The personal blog must support real estate insights, buying guides, investment, market trends, architecture and company updates. Use reusable components and structured property/blog data, preserve the existing technology stack, inspect the codebase first, implement directly rather than merely suggesting, test all routes and interactions, use realistic Nigerian Naira pricing where applicable, and never invent company facts, awards, testimonials or legal guarantees. Deliver a complete production-quality real estate sales website, not an Airbnb reskin.
